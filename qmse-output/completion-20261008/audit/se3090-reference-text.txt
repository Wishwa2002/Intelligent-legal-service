PART A – GROUP REPORT
1.0 Project Overview
Project Identity: LegalEase AI – Enterprise Omnichannel Legal Practice Management & Autonomous Multi-Agent AI Subsystem.
What LegalEase AI Is: A secure, multi-tier enterprise legal practice management platform integrating ASP.NET Core 8 Web API, PostgreSQL (Neon Cloud), React 19 web application, Flutter 3 cross-platform mobile client, and a decoupled Python FastAPI multi-agent AI reasoning mesh.
Real-World Problem Solved: Eliminates administrative bottlenecks in legal service discovery, attorney matching, consultation scheduling, and statutory document verification.
Target Users: Law firm Managing Partners (Administrators), Senior Legal Clerks, Practicing Lawyers (Attorneys), and Corporate/Individual Clients.
Why the System is Useful: Provides transparent fee structures, conflict-free automated appointment scheduling, AI-assisted document filing compliance, and 24/7 conversational preliminary legal triage.
Main Technologies: C# / ASP.NET Core 8 Web API, Entity Framework Core 8, PostgreSQL 16 (Neon Cloud), React 19, TypeScript, TailwindCSS v4, Flutter 3.x, Python 3.11, FastAPI, Google Gemini 1.5/2.0 Flash, Playwright, xUnit.
High-Level Features: Role-based authentication with JWT; dynamic lawyer directory & specializations; 12 statutory legal documentation templates; conflict-free calendar booking; multi-agent AI consultation and matching; mobile & web responsive parity.
2.0 Problem Statement
The traditional legal services industry in Sri Lanka and globally suffers from acute systemic inefficiencies across multiple operational dimensions:
Difficulty Discovering Specialized Legal Counsel: Clients facing specialized legal dilemmas (e.g. partition actions, IP infringement, tax disputes) lack reliable mechanisms to discover vetted practitioners with appropriate bar seniority and domain expertise.
Opaque Fee Structures & Consultation Ambiguity: Clients encounter unexpected consultation fees and ambiguous billing practices, discouraging early legal consultation and worsening disputes.
Inefficient Appointment & Reservation Management: Law firms rely on fragmented telephone bookings and manual diaries, leading to double-booking of busy attorneys, scheduling conflicts with court dates, and missed client consultations.
Paper-Heavy Documentation & Verification Bottlenecks: Filing legal documents (Deeds of Transfer, Last Wills, Affidavits) requires manual document submissions, where incomplete filings cause prolonged delays and statutory rejections.
Staff & Clerk Operational Disconnect: Legal clerks, managing partners, and practicing attorneys operate in disconnected silos without centralized audit queues, caseload balancing, or transparent workflow tracking.
Lack of Integrated Intelligent Decision-Making: Traditional law firm portals lack autonomous artificial intelligence to assist clients with preliminary legal triage, statutory requirement checklists, or data-driven workforce planning.
LegalEase AI resolves these challenges through an integrated digital platform that combines deterministic database transaction integrity with autonomous multi-agent reasoning, streamlining legal workflows from intake to certified execution.
3.0 Project Objectives
The engineering of LegalEase AI is guided by concrete, quantifiable business and technical objectives:
Obj-1 (Centralized Governance): Consolidate 100% of law firm administrative operations—lawyer management, appointment scheduling, document verification, and client service requests—into a unified role-gated web and mobile ecosystem.
Obj-2 (Autonomous AI Triage & Grounding): Deploy a multi-agent AI subsystem leveraging Google Gemini and hybrid RAG to provide preliminary legal guidance, statutory checklist verification, and candidate lawyer recommendations with >95% intent classification accuracy.
Obj-3 (Zero-Conflict Scheduling): Guarantee 100% conflict-free appointment booking using atomic database locking and unique compound constraints on `(LawyerId, AppointmentDate)`.
Obj-4 (Statutory Document Compliance): Standardize 12 statutory Sri Lankan legal documentation filing workflows with dynamic prerequisite checklist preview and mandatory human-in-the-loop clerk verification.
Obj-5 (High-Throughput Enterprise Architecture): Maintain sub-200ms API response latency (p95) for transactional operations and sub-1.5s response latency for complex multi-agent AI inferences.
Obj-6 (Robust Software Quality): Achieve a 100% active test pass rate across 281 automated xUnit backend test cases and 14 Playwright web browser E2E test suites.

7.1 Cross-Platform End-to-End Workflow
The specification requires at least one complete workflow demonstrating end-to-end integration across both clients, the backend, database, and AI service:
Phase 1 (Mobile Client Intake): A client opens the Flutter mobile app, logs in, and engages the Common AI Legal Advisor describing a property encroachment issue. The AI analyzes the inquiry, categorizes it as Property Law, and suggests filing a Deed of Transfer request.
Phase 2 (Document Submission): The client navigates to the Document Services hub, selects Deed of Transfer, and uploads copies of the Title Deed and Survey Plan via the mobile file picker.
Phase 3 (AI Verification & Scoring): ASP.NET Core forwards the document metadata to the Document AI Agent (port 8001). The agent audits the attachments against the 4 statutory checklist items and assigns a 100% completeness score.
Phase 4 (Web Clerk Review & Human Sign-Off): A Senior Legal Clerk logs into the React web app (`/clerk/cases`), reviews the uploaded attachments, verifies the AI checklist status, and clicks 'Approve Filing'.
Phase 5 (Lawyer Booking & Status Synchronization): The client receives an updated status notification on their mobile dashboard and proceeds to book a consultation with the recommended property attorney.
8.0 Database Design & Relational ER Model
The relational database is implemented on PostgreSQL 16 (Neon Cloud) using Entity Framework Core 8 Code-First migrations. The schema is fully normalized to Third Normal Form (3NF) and comprises 11 core tables:
Table Name | Primary Key | Foreign Keys & Relations | Key Columns & Constraints
Users | Id (INT) | None | Email (UQ), PasswordHash, FullName, Role, CreatedAt, IsActive
Specializations | Id (INT) | None | Name (UQ), Description, Icon, IsActive
Lawyers | Id (INT) | SpecializationId -> Specializations.Id, UserId -> Users.Id | FullName, BarNumber (UQ), Email, Phone, ExperienceYears, HourlyRate, IsAvailable
LegalServices | Id (INT) | SpecializationId -> Specializations.Id | Title, Description, SpecializationId, DurationMinutes, Price, IsActive
Appointments | Id (INT) | LawyerId -> Lawyers.Id, ClientId -> Users.Id | LawyerId, ClientId, AppointmentDate, Status, Notes, UQ(LawyerId, AppointmentDate)
Careers | Id (INT) | None | Title, Department, Location, Type, Description, Requirements, IsActive
Clerks | Id (INT) | UserId -> Users.Id | FullName, Email, Phone, Department, ActiveCasesCount, IsAvailable
DocumentationServices | Id (INT) | None | Name (UQ), Category, Description, RequiredDocuments (JSON), FilingFee
DocumentationRequests | Id (INT) | ServiceId -> DocumentationServices.Id, ClientId -> Users.Id, ClerkId -> Clerks.Id | TrackingNumber (UQ), Status, SubmissionDate, CompletionDate, Remarks
DocumentFiles | Id (INT) | DocumentationRequestId -> DocumentationRequests.Id | FileName, FilePath, FileSize, MimeType, UploadedAt
ServiceRequests | Id (INT) | ClientId -> Users.Id | Title, Description, Category, Status, CreatedAt, Priority

8.1 Database Normalization & Indexing Strategy
All repeating groups, partial dependencies, and transitive dependencies are eliminated, fulfilling 3NF standards. B-Tree indexes are applied on `Lawyers.SpecializationId`, `Appointments(LawyerId, AppointmentDate)`, `DocumentFiles.DocumentationRequestId`, and `Users.Email` to ensure O(log N) lookup speeds.
9.0 ASP.NET Core Web API Design
The backend is built with ASP.NET Core 8 following Clean Architecture principles, partitioned into Controllers, Application Services, Data Repositories, and Data Transfer Objects (DTOs):
Controllers: `AuthController`, `LawyersController`, `SpecializationsController`, `LegalServicesController`, `AppointmentsController`, `CareersController`, `DocumentationServicesController`, `DocumentationRequestsController`, `ClerksController`, `ServiceRequestsController`, `ClientsController`.
DTO Encapsulation: Strict boundary enforcement ensures EF Core entities are never exposed directly to HTTP consumers. Request and Response DTOs carry validation attributes (`[Required]`, `[EmailAddress]`, `[StringLength]`).
Dependency Injection: Services and DbContext are registered with scoped lifetimes in `Program.cs` (`AddScoped<IAppointmentService, AppointmentService>()`).
Centralized Exception Handling: Custom `ExceptionMiddleware` intercepts unhandled exceptions, logs errors securely, and outputs RFC 7807 Problem Details JSON.
Interactive Swagger Documentation: Configured with Swashbuckle OpenAPI, providing interactive endpoint documentation at `/swagger/index.html` with Bearer token authentication support.
10.0 React Web Application Design
The web frontend is built with React 19, Vite, TypeScript, and TailwindCSS v4. It features client-side routing via React Router 7, centralized authentication state management via `authApi` and browser localStorage, and role-gated navigation:
Folder Architecture: `src/pages/`, `src/components/`, `src/api/`, `src/context/`, `src/types/`, `src/hooks/`.
Protected Route Guarding: Declarative `ProtectedRoute` wrappers inspect decoded JWT roles and automatically redirect unauthenticated or unauthorized users.
State Management & API Layer: Axios instances with interceptors automatically attach JWT Bearer tokens to outgoing requests and handle 401 unauthorized token expirations.
Responsive Legal Aesthetic: Engineered with a high-trust color palette consisting of Deep Navy (`#0B1E3F`), Rich Gold (`#C9A75C`), Slate Blue, and clean whites.

14.0 Security Considerations
JWT Cryptographic Signing: HMAC-SHA256 signed tokens with 24-hour expiration carrying sub, email, and role claims.
Password Security: BCrypt hashing with 12 salt rounds; zero plaintext password storage.
Role-Based Access Control: ASP.NET Core `[Authorize(Roles = ...)]` attributes on all sensitive controller actions.
SQL Injection Prevention: Entity Framework Core uses parameterized SQL queries exclusively, eliminating SQL injection vectors.
Agentic AI Injection Defense: `injection_defense.py` sanitizes user prompts and rejects adversarial jailbreak attempts before LLM processing.
File Upload Restrictions: MIME type verification, 10MB ceiling, and storage outside public web directory.

20.0 Architecture Decision Records (ADRs)
ADR-001: Structured Multi-Agent LLM Orchestration with Gemini Grounding
Status: Accepted
Context: The platform requires autonomous intelligence across 4 functional domains. Monolithic prompts lack determinism.
Options Considered: 1. Direct client-side OpenAI API calls | 2. Python FastAPI multi-agent microservice mesh | 3. Native C# Semantic Kernel.
Decision: Adopted Option 2: Python FastAPI microservices (ports 8001 & 8002) with Pydantic JSON schemas calling Gemini 1.5/2.0 Flash.
Consequences: Guarantees 100% schema-valid JSON outputs; isolates AI compute; requires orchestrating internal service network.
ADR-002: React 19 Frontend with TailwindCSS v4 and Centralized Auth Persistence
Status: Accepted
Context: Fast, responsive single-page web portal needed for Admins, Clerks, Lawyers, and Clients.
Options Considered: 1. Next.js SSR | 2. React 19 SPA with Vite | 3. Angular 18.
Decision: Adopted Option 2: React 19 with Vite, TypeScript, React Router 7, and TailwindCSS v4.
Consequences: Lightning-fast HMR (<300ms); zero CSS bloat; requires disciplined client-side token invalidation upon logout.
ADR-003: Flutter Cross-Platform Architecture with Dynamic AI Hub Routing
Status: Accepted
Context: Mobile users require cross-platform Android/iOS support for consultations and document tracking.
Options Considered: 1. React Native | 2. Flutter 3.x | 3. Separate Native Android (Kotlin) & iOS (Swift).
Decision: Adopted Option 2: Flutter 3.x with shared Dart codebase and native compilation.
Consequences: Single codebase; native 60fps performance; emulator requires loopback IP `10.0.2.2` for local backend.
ADR-004: Relational PostgreSQL Schema via EF Core 8 with Code-First Migrations
Status: Accepted
Context: Legal records, billing rates, appointments, and filings require strict ACID guarantees.
Options Considered: 1. MongoDB Document DB | 2. PostgreSQL 16 on Neon Cloud via EF Core 8 | 3. MySQL.
Decision: Adopted Option 2: PostgreSQL 16 on Neon Cloud with EF Core 8 Code-First migrations.
Consequences: Total relational integrity; automated schema synchronization across team members; requires formal migrations.
ADR-005: Decoupled Microservice Mesh with Internal AI Compute Isolation
Status: Accepted
Context: Python AI libraries (LangChain, LangGraph) must not crash core transactional legal APIs.
Options Considered: 1. Monolithic .NET backend with Python subprocesses | 2. Decoupled microservice mesh | 3. Serverless Lambda functions.
Decision: Adopted Option 2: Decoupled Python FastAPI services called strictly through ASP.NET Core internal HTTP REST.
Consequences: Total fault isolation; AI crashes do not affect booking APIs; requires multiple service startup scripts.

2.3 Component 1 Autonomous AI Agent: Lawyer Recommendation & Workforce Intelligence Agent
As the lead for Component 1, I engineered the Lawyer Recommendation & Workforce Intelligence Agent. This autonomous subsystem runs as a dedicated Python FastAPI microservice on port 8002 (`ai-service/lawyer_recommendation/agent.py`, `gemini.py`, and `workforce_hiring/drafting.py`) and is invoked internally by the ASP.NET Core `LawyerRecommendationsController`.
📌 Component 1 AI Agent: Architectural Specification
• Agent Name: Lawyer Recommendation & Workforce Intelligence Agent
• Implementation Engine: Python 3.11 + FastAPI + Google Gemini 1.5/2.0 Flash
• Core Source Files: lawyer_recommendation/agent.py, gemini.py, workforce_hiring/drafting.py
• Pipeline Position: Downstream matching & capacity planning agent in the legal intake mesh
• Safety Protocol: Physically Read-Only; recommendations are advisory; client retains sole booking authority.

Detailed Multi-Stage Autonomous Execution Lifecycle:
Stage 1 (Client Narrative Intake & Feature Extraction): Ingests client legal narratives via `POST /api/lawyer-recommendations/match`, extracting target practice category, estimated dispute complexity, and client urgency level.
Stage 2 (Candidate Pool Vector Query): Queries active attorneys from the PostgreSQL `Lawyers` repository filtered by specialization (Corporate, Criminal, Property, Labour, Tax Law), pulling bar seniority, win rates, and current active case quotas.
Stage 3 (Multi-Criteria Mathematical Scoring Engine): Calculates an empirical composite fitness score bounded in [0.0, 100.0]:
Fitness Score = (0.40 × PracticeAreaFit) + (0.30 × BarSeniorityScore) + (0.20 × CaseloadHeadroom) + (0.10 × ClientRating).
Stage 4 (Gemini 1.5/2.0 Grounded Reasoning): Transmits the top candidate feature tuples and client narrative to Google Gemini with few-shot prompts, instructing the model to synthesize a professional, evidence-backed legal justification explaining why each attorney is suited to the dispute.
Stage 5 (Pydantic Schema Serialization): Serializes output into strictly validated `LawyerMatchResponse` JSON models (`extra='forbid'`), enforcing schema conformity before returning to ASP.NET Core.
Stage 6 (Workforce Intelligence & Capacity Forecasting): In the background, `workforce_hiring/drafting.py` analyzes rolling 30-day case influx across practice categories. When attorney caseload headroom drops below 20%, it autonomously drafts formal recruitment proposals for firm administrators.

name: Backend CI
on: [push, pull_request]
jobs:
  build-and-test:
    runs-on: ubuntu-latest
    steps:
    - uses: actions/checkout@v4
    - name: Setup .NET 8
      uses: actions/setup-dotnet@v4
      with:
        dotnet-version: '8.0.x'
    - name: Restore dependencies
      run: dotnet restore backend/LegalService.sln
    - name: Build
      run: dotnet build backend/LegalService.sln --no-restore
    - name: Run xUnit Tests
      run: dotnet test backend/LegalService.Tests/LegalService.Tests.csproj --no-build --verbosity normal

Appendix E: OpenAPI (Swagger) Endpoint Contracts Summary
Summary of core REST endpoints exposed by ASP.NET Core 8 Web API:
Controller | HTTP Route | Primary Responsibilities
AuthController | POST /api/auth/login, POST /api/auth/register | Issues JWT HMAC-SHA256 tokens and registers new staff/clients.
LawyersController | GET /api/lawyers, POST /api/lawyers, PUT /api/lawyers/{id} | Lawyer CRUD, bar number validation, specialization filtering.
AppointmentsController | GET /api/appointments, POST /api/appointments | Booking management, calendar slot availability, atomic commits.
DocumentationRequestsController | GET /api/documentation-requests, POST /api/documentation-requests | Document request lifecycle, file uploads, checklist audits.
ClerksController | GET /api/clerks, POST /api/clerks, PUT /api/clerks/{id} | Clerk staff profiles, department assignments, active case counts.
ServiceRequestsController | GET /api/service-requests, POST /api/service-requests | Client case intake, triage audit queue, status transitions.
